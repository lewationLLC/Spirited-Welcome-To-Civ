# Death Valley — Cutscene Design Brief

**Format:** cutscene (not a playable area).

## What it is

Death Valley is the great cleft that splits the mountain range between the
**Wildlands of the West** and **the Breach**. It was carved by the arrow
whose head is now **the Arrowhead** (see [`../arrowhead/`](../arrowhead/README.md)):
fired and falling from the sky, the arrow split the mountain range in two
on its way down.

## Role in the story

**Jaun** travels through Death Valley to reach the **Great Gate** and **the
Breach** (see [`../great-gate/`](../great-gate/README.md)). The cutscene
covers that crossing and ends as the valley opens out onto the Breach's
cleared farmland, with the Great Gate in view — i.e. it hands off to the
distant-approach shot in `../great-gate/approach-distant.jpg`.

Travel order: Wildlands of the West → **Death Valley** → buffer farmland →
Great Gate → the Breach.

## Look

- **Shape:** a long, straight, unnaturally clean cut through the mountains —
  not a river valley. Sheer, smooth walls on both sides, as if sliced, with
  the line of the cut running dead straight toward the horizon along the
  arrow's flight path.
- **Walls:** split rock faces showing the mountain's layered interior; some
  sections fused or glassy where the arrow's passage scorched them, with
  traces of black obsidian glinting in the stone.
- **Floor:** a narrow, barren, rubble-strewn floor of fallen rock and dust.
  Little or nothing grows — it should earn the name.
- **Light:** a thin strip of sky high above; the floor is in deep shadow for
  most of the day, with shafts of light where the cut widens.
- **Mood:** desolate, dangerous and awe-inspiring — the scar of something
  impossibly powerful. A sense of relief when it opens onto the green
  farmland and the Great Gate.
